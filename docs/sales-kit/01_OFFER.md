# The Offer — Aleefy for Egyptian clinics

**This folder is for selling TO CLINICS.**
`docs/sale/` is a different thing entirely — that one is for selling the whole
asset to a single acquirer. Do not mix them up in front of a customer.

---

## 1. Why the original offer needed changing

The plan was: **7,000 EGP, 6 months support, 3 free change requests.**

Two things break it.

### You get paid once and support forever

| | Month 1 | Month 7 | Month 24 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| You receive | 7,000 | 0 | 0 |
| Clinic still expects | setup, training | fixes, questions | fixes, questions |

Ten clinics on this model is 70,000 EGP **in total, ever**, and ten clinics with
your personal phone number. At month 12 you are working full time for money you
were paid a year ago.

The fix is not a higher price. It is **naming the renewal at the start**, when
nobody minds, instead of at month 7, when it sounds like a new bill.

### "3 change requests" has no ceiling

A change request can be twenty minutes or three weeks. Nothing in the wording
stops a clinic asking for *"a small change — make it talk to my accountant's
program."* You then either work for free or start the relationship with an
argument.

The fix is to sell **hours, not requests**. A capped hour lets you say
"that one is 14 hours, so it's the free 8 plus 6 billed" without it being
personal.

## 2. The offer to actually use

### Option A — Licence (رخصة دائمة)

The one most Egyptian clinics will recognise and prefer.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Licence** | **7,000 EGP**, one time |
| Support | **6 months included** — phone, WhatsApp, remote fixes |
| Changes | **3 requests, up to 8 hours each** (24 hours total) |
| Data migration | **Free** — your Excel files loaded before you pay |
| Training | 2 sessions for your staff |
| **From month 7** | **1,800 EGP / year** — support, updates, new features |

The renewal is on the page from day one. It is not a surprise, and it is not
optional if they want support — but the software keeps working if they refuse.
Say that out loud; it removes the fear of being held hostage.

### Option B — Subscription (اشتراك شهري)

For a clinic that does not have 7,000 EGP today. You now have multi-tenancy, so
this costs you almost nothing extra to run.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Monthly** | **600 EGP / month** |
| Included | Everything — support, updates, migration, training |
| Upfront | **Nothing** |
| Exit | Cancel any month. Your data exports to Excel and it is yours. |

600/month passes 7,000 at month 12, and by year three it is more than double
the licence. Offer both honestly and let them choose — the clinic that picks
monthly is usually the one that would never have paid 7,000 at all.

### Option C — Founding clinics (أول 3 عيادات)

**Not a discount. A trade.** You need proof more than you need 21,000 EGP.

**They get:** one full year free, licence included, priority support.

**You get, in writing, before it starts:**
1. A short video of the vet saying what the system does for them
2. Permission to use the clinic's name and photo in your material
3. Their real numbers after 3 months — no-shows before and after, hours saved
4. Two introductions to other clinic owners

Cap it at three, and say the cap out loud. It is what makes it an opportunity
rather than a sign you are desperate.

## 3. What counts as a change request

Put this in the contract. It is the difference between a customer and a
dependent.

**Included in the 8 hours:**
- New report or a change to an existing one
- New fields on a form
- Price list, service catalogue, tax rate changes
- Adjusting a printed document — invoice, receipt, certificate
- New user roles or permission changes
- Arabic wording changes

**Not included (quoted separately):**
- Connecting to another company's software (accounting, lab machines, government portals)
- A new module that does not exist yet
- Anything needing a new device, printer driver or hardware
- Data migration from a second system after the first is done
- Work caused by the clinic's own internet, PC or Windows problems

**How it works:** the clinic writes the request on WhatsApp. You reply with an
hour estimate **before** starting. They confirm. Nothing begins without a
confirmed estimate, so there is never a disagreement afterwards.

## 4. Payment terms

- **50% to start, 50% on the day it goes live.** Never 100% up front (they will
  not trust it) and never 100% at the end (you will chase it).
- Renewal invoiced **one month before** the anniversary.
- If renewal lapses: the software keeps running, support stops. Never disable a
  clinic's system over money. It is their patient records, it will destroy your
  name in a market this small, and word travels between clinics faster than any
  advertising you could buy.

## 5. What NOT to promise

Read this before every meeting.

| Do not say | Why |
|---|---|
| "X clinics use it" | Zero clinics use it. One sentence like this, discovered later, ends the relationship. |
| "The drug checker will catch dangerous combinations" | The rule set ships marked DRAFT and is not reviewed by a licensed vet. Four of its six drug classes are referenced by no rule. **Do not sell the CDS module.** Do not demo it. |
| "It works offline" | It needs the server running. |
| "It connects to your accountant's software" | Nothing is built for this. Quote it as custom work. |
| Anything about WhatsApp, until you have sent one yourself | See below. |

**WhatsApp is your best feature and it has never sent a real message.**
`wapilot settings rows: 0`. Connect it and send one reminder to your own phone
before you show it to anybody. If it fails in a meeting you lose the meeting,
and it is the feature they came to see.

The last one is the one that actually wins deals. Arabic inside a generated PDF
is where nearly every competing system fails — the letters break apart or come
out backwards. **Print an invoice in front of them.** It takes ten seconds and
it is the most convincing thing you own.
