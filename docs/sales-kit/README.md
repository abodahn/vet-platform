## Before you send the one-pager

Open `02_ONE_PAGER_AR.html` in a browser and replace the four yellow
highlighted placeholders — your name, phone, email and the offer expiry date.
They are highlighted precisely so they cannot be missed. Then **Ctrl+P → Save as
PDF** and send that.

## The three things that decide everything

1. **Load their real data before the demo.** A vet seeing their own patients on
   screen is the entire sale. Ask for the Excel file two days early.
2. **Send a real WhatsApp message before you demo it.** As of today
   `wapilot settings rows: 0` — nothing has ever been sent end to end. It is the
   feature they came to see and the one most likely to embarrass you.
3. **Never demo the CDS module.** It ships marked
   `DRAFT — NOT REVIEWED BY A LICENSED VETERINARIAN`, and four of its six drug
   classes are referenced by no rule, so real combinations return no warning.
   That is a liability, not a feature.
